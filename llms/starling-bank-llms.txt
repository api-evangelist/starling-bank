# Starling Bank

> Starling Bank is a UK app-only digital challenger bank (founded 2014, London) holding a full UK banking licence, regulated by the PRA and FCA. Its primary developer surface is a bespoke, developer-friendly RESTful Developer API (accounts, transaction feed, payments, payees, savings goals/spaces, cards, mandates, identity) secured with OAuth2 at api.starlingbank.com, with a full sandbox. As an FCA-authorised ASPSP it also conforms to the UK Open Banking (OBIE) Read/Write standard (AIS/PIS/CBPII) secured with FAPI OAuth2/OIDC, mutual-TLS, and PSD2 SCA.

## APIs
- [Starling Accounts API](https://developer.starlingbank.com/docs): accounts, identifiers, balances (OAuth2, base https://api.starlingbank.com/api/v2)
- [Starling Account Holder API](https://developer.starlingbank.com/docs): account-holder profile/type
- [Starling Transaction Feed API](https://developer.starlingbank.com/docs): real-time feed items, categories, receipts
- [Starling Payments API](https://developer.starlingbank.com/docs): domestic/scheduled payments, standing orders
- [Starling Payees API](https://developer.starlingbank.com/docs): payees and payee accounts
- [Starling Savings Goals API](https://developer.starlingbank.com/docs): savings goals/spaces, round-ups
- [Starling Cards API](https://developer.starlingbank.com/docs): card details and per-feature controls
- [Starling Mandates API](https://developer.starlingbank.com/docs): direct debit mandates
- [Starling Identity API](https://developer.starlingbank.com/docs): authenticated identity and granted token scopes
- [Open Banking Read/Write](https://developer.starlingbank.com/docs/open-banking): AIS, PIS, CBPII (OBIE v3.1, FAPI + mTLS + PSD2 SCA, TPP-provisioned)

## Auth
- OAuth2 authorization endpoint: https://oauth.starlingbank.com
- OAuth2 token endpoint: https://token-api.starlingbank.com/oauth/access-token
- Authorization-server metadata: https://api.starlingbank.com/.well-known/oauth-authorization-server
- Sandbox host: https://api-sandbox.starlingbank.com/api/v2
